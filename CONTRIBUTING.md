## 🎯 Why people use it

Windows and most modern applications describe your machine using a set of unique identifiers — disk serials, MAC addresses, motherboard strings, BIOS UUIDs, and so on. That's fine for everyday use, but sometimes it becomes a headache. A fresh SSD or a new Wi‑Fi card can throw driver installs into disarray. Software trials and test environments can get confused by identifiers left over from an old setup. And if you care about privacy, you may simply not want every app on your PC reading the same fingerprints every day. This utility gives you a straightforward way to look at those identifiers, save them, and update them when needed.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this safe to run?**  
Yes — it only touches user‑level identifiers and never harms hardware. That said, it's still a system‑level utility, so always make a restore point and save your originals first.
